The purpose

Why this library exists

Most videos are scored in the final hour, by someone typing “upbeat corporate” into a stock site. The trouble is rarely taste. It is vocabulary.

You hear the right music in your head, but without its name you cannot ask for it: not from a composer, not from an AI music model, not from a search bar. So you settle for whatever the algorithm hands back.

This library teaches the names by ear. Every record was composed for the films technology companies actually make: launch films, teasers, product demos, social reels, founder letters. Each is taken apart while it plays, so you can watch the kick arrive, hear the silence before the drop, and read what that moment is called.

  4. The brief. Ready to paste into any tool, or send to a composer.
