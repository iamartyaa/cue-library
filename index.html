The Cue Library

A reading room for the music of moving pictures

The listening boothThe stacksThe card catalogue

Engineers can direct a machine on code because they know the words. This library gives you the same power over music. Borrow a record, watch every part of the band move on the green baize, and read the name of each moment as it happens. Then write your brief like someone who has spent years in the studio.

  1. i.Borrow a record from the stacks. It is carried to the turntable in the listening booth.
  2. ii.Watch the score. Each colour is one part of the band; the typed card names each moment.
  3. iii.Take the call slip. Copy the brief and prompt, and hand them to any AI music tool or editor.
